Plan your shots

Plan the photos you want to take on a trip, and track them as you go.

  • Click + New shot list to add a trip with dates and the shots you want

  • Filter lists by upcoming, in progress, or completed
  • Edit or delete a list anytime
  • See your overall progress in the statistics at the bottom
